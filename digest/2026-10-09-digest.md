# Newsletter digest, week of 2026-10-09

## THE ONE THING
A counter-thesis to "personal agents" surfaced this week: a guest essay in The Leverage argues shared, purpose-built AI "factories" (specific, scrutinized systems many people use) beat individual personal agents for business work, and Ben's Bites endorsed the same essay (High confidence on what was argued; "factories win" is the author's thesis, not an established fact). This complicates "agent persistence," escalated for 5 runs, even as personal agents kept proliferating (Hark, Tab at $300M, Grok Bot switching its primary model to Claude Opus 5.5). My extension: before expanding your own agent setup, consider whether a shared, trackable system for one recurring task would serve better.
