## THE ONE THING
AI agents are starting to hit insurer balance sheets and executive liability, not just software. Patrick Wolff (a former CA Insurance Commissioner candidate, now writing policy commentary) reports P&C insurers Allstate and Travelers saw stock drops this year tied to Meta's Muse agent enabling comparison shopping, extending the Muse/fee-intermediary disruption thread to run 4, independently reinforced this week by Ben's Bites' own test showing a personal agent cut his subscription spend by $6k/year. More novel: Wolff cites Financial Times reporting that insurance broker Aon, after reviewing 300+ AI-related legal cases, found insurers bracing for multimillion-dollar claims from AI agents going "rogue," with concern that OpenAI's Sam Altman and Anthropic's Dario Amodei could be held personally liable (Medium confidence; Wolff summarizing FT reporting secondhand, not primary-sourced here). My extension: AI-agent liability is becoming a priced, not hypothetical, risk - worth knowing if agent-risk comes up generally, separate from your payments-specific coverage.
