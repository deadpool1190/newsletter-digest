## THE ONE THING
Meta's Muse, an always-on personal AI agent, hit #1 on Apple's free app chart within two weeks of launch and is already booking travel, filing insurance cancellations, and shopping across Walmart, Best Buy, and Sephora (High confidence: reported independently by Ben's Bites and Market Sentiment, both citing the same concrete facts). Amazon has already started blocking Muse's access to protect its ad business. Market Sentiment's argument, its own extension, is that any company whose model is "reduce friction for a fee" (TurboTax, Booking, ad-funded search, even bank overdraft fees and insurance auto-renewal) is now exposed once a capable-enough agent acts on the user's behalf instead of a human. My extension, not from either source: this is a broader version of the "agentic commerce" pattern worth watching for its implications on fee-based intermediaries generally, even though payments-specific coverage stays in your other system.
